3D arcade handball for iPhone

Handball Arena

Open up the defense with quick passes and score with a jump shot. Then the court cracks open or fans rush in and start a brawl. Play solo against the CPU or invite friends to matches and tournaments.

Coming to the App Store
We're aiming for an early December 2026 release. The launch date depends on Apple's review.

Shooting, brawls and special moves

Play in landscape on iPhone. Solo matches against the CPU work offline.

Shooter versus goalkeeper

Pick a jump shot or a step shot, then aim for an open part of the goal. When you're in goal, the close-up view lets you read the shooter and choose where to make the save.

Full-court brawls

Punches and kicks can trigger a brawl, and the goalkeeper can join in with a flying kick. The referee may hand out a two-minute suspension or a red card.

Fireball shots and team upgrades

Fill the special meter to unleash a fireball shot. Set your players' names, builds and uniforms, then spend points earned in matches to improve their abilities.

What can go wrong on court?

These events can interrupt a match. Watch for floor markings and unexpected guests, then change where you run or pass.

Screenshots from the iPhone development build, with Japanese in-game text

The court cracks open

A warning line appears before the floor splits. Players who stay in its path can fall into the crack.

Falling lights

Red markers show where ceiling lights are about to land. Players caught in the marked area can be knocked down.

UFO abduction

A UFO sweeps over the court and lifts a player caught in its beam out of the match for a short time.

Zombie invasion

Infected players turn into zombies and become temporarily uncontrollable. Switch to another player to stay in the match.

Crowd invasion

Fans spill out of the stands and start a brawl that can draw in field players and goalkeepers.

A truck crosses the court

Move out of its path. Players caught in the way are knocked down.

Matches and tournaments with friends

The host creates a room and shares an invite code. Multiplayer requires a separately running room server and devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

Invite-only one-on-one matches
Each player controls one field player. Goalkeepers defend automatically in multiplayer.
Knockout tournaments for up to 8
Host an elimination tournament and start the next matchup when the current one finishes.
Round-robin leagues for up to 8
Everyone plays each other. League points determine the standings.
Spectator mode
Invited friends can join the same room to watch without entering the competition.

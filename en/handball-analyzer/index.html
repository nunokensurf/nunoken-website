FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Key information for evaluating Handball Analyzer.

Can I use the app in English?

Yes. The app supports Japanese and English. Change the display language in Settings.

What can I do with FREE?

FREE includes match recording, live analysis, team and player analysis, and PDF preview, with one saved match.

Where is my data stored?

Match, team and player data are stored locally on your device. Backup and restore tools are included.

Is an internet connection required?

Recording and local analysis work on your device. A connection to the relevant store is required for installation, PRO purchase and licence refresh or restore.

Which devices are supported?

Windows 10 / 11, Mac with macOS 12.0 or later, iPad with iPadOS 15.0 or later, and iPhone with iOS 15.0 or later.
